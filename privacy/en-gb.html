Privacy Policy Updated on 19 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Kitta application ("we", "our", or "app"), maintained by independent developer Joanatas Martins, protects and processes user data in full compliance with data protection laws (including the UK GDPR, Data Protection Act 2018, and EU GDPR) and Google Play Developer Policies.

1. Local Storage and Data Sovereignty

Kitta adopts a 100% Local Storage architecture. All records you add — including bill names, payees, monetary amounts, due dates, notes, categories, and income entries — are stored exclusively in the local SQLite database inside your mobile device's secure internal storage.

We do not operate proprietary cloud synchronisation servers, and we have no access, possession, or visibility of any of your financial information.

2. Connected Services & Technology Providers

To enable distribution and app sustainability, we integrate libraries from trusted industry partners:

  • Google AdMob (Free Version): Displays non-intrusive banner ads in the free version. Google AdMob SDK may collect anonymous device identifiers (Google Advertising ID) and basic telemetry for fraud prevention and ad delivery. See the Google Advertising Policies.
  • Google Play Billing & RevenueCat (Kitta Pro): Subscription processing and billing are handled exclusively by Google Play. RevenueCat only validates the cryptographic store receipt. We never see or store your credit card or bank account details.

When you subscribe to Kitta Pro, all advertising SDK calls are immediately deactivated.

3. Android Permissions Requested

Permission Purpose in the App
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Display local notifications in the system notification shade to alert you about due or overdue bills.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM Schedule due date reminders at the exact time chosen by the user, ensuring timely alerts even in power-saving modes.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Restore and reschedule active local reminders if the device is rebooted or restarted.
INTERNET Required to load AdMob banners (in the free tier) and validate the Kitta Pro purchase receipt with Google Play.

4. Permanent Data Deletion

Because your data does not reside on external cloud servers, you maintain complete sovereignty:

  • You can delete any bill or income entry at any time inside the app.
  • To permanently erase all data, simply uninstall Kitta from your device or select "Clear Storage / Clear Data" in your Android app settings.
